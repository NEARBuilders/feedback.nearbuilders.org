The feedback editor no longer silently drops text past the length limit: what you see is what gets posted, and going over the limit shows how much to cut and blocks posting. The limit is now 30,000 visible characters, and posting is blocked while an image hasn't finished uploading. Round consoles get a Testers tab: everyone who posted, with their wallet, Telegram handle and open count, and one click to read all of a tester's feedback and resolve it together.
